Bullion & Precious Metal

Buy Locally. Store Securely. Access Anytime.

Store your gold, silver, and other precious metals in a segregated custody account, physically separate from every other customer's holdings — so you're guaranteed to receive back the exact items you stored.

We also accept direct shipment delivery for bulk storage, so your metals arrive straight into our vault.

Sourcing, Without the Risk

Mountain Vault works with reputable local precious-metals sellers to help clients source gold and silver without the delays, risk, or cost of shipping. Once purchased, your metals go directly into our secure private vault — never in the mail, never left unattended.

Mountain Vault does not sell precious metals and does not act as a dealer or broker. We connect clients with reputable local sellers and provide secure, private vault storage once metals are acquired.
