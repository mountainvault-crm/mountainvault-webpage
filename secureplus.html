SecurePlus

Arizona's first SDBIC®-accredited private vault facility — insurance built in, not bolted on.

Mountain Vault is the first private vault facility in Arizona accredited under SecurePlus® — an SDBIC® program underwritten by AXA Art, a specialist in insuring high-value items in storage facilities and part of the AXA Insurance group, one of the largest insurers in the world.

To earn accreditation, Mountain Vault underwent an independent evaluation of the safety of the property stored in our facility.
